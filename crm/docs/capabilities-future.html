Designed, not built

Parked · September 2026

Busy time mirrored across your Outlook calendars

Outlook cannot block one account's calendar when a meeting lands in another, and Alex holds three accounts. The CRM already reads every connected calendar, so it can do the job: a per-mailbox switch under Settings, Email accounts ("Block time here when another of my calendars is busy"). A meeting booked in one mailbox gets a private "Busy" placeholder in the others, kept in step when the original moves and removed when it is cancelled. Free-time entries and meetings already on both calendars are left alone. Accounts without an Exchange calendar (the brightday address) cannot take part.

To switch on: about a day of work, no cost. Say "build the busy mirror".

Parked · September 2026

A staging copy of the CRM

A second container on the same VPS, crm-staging.brightday.com.au, with its own database and sample data, an amber Staging mark on every screen, and no email or webhooks. Every pull request would be pushed to a staging branch and rebuilt there within five minutes, so a change can be clicked through before it is merged and goes live. Built and tested; parked before the one-time setup (DNS, Cloudflare Access, two Entra redirect URIs, a compose service and a Caddy block).

To switch on: reopen pull request #76, merge it, then follow deploy/STAGING.md. About an hour of setup, no extra hosting cost.

Proposed · September 2026

Unlisted funds in model figures

A model that holds an unlisted fund (an APIR code) leaves that fund out of its growth chart and its return and risk figures, because the fund has no daily price. The Morningstar reference data already carries each fund's one, three, five and ten year returns, three-year standard deviation and fees, so the model's weighted figures could include it, labelled as estimates alongside the price-based figures. About a day.

Why it was parked

Waiting on priority, not on a blocker

The busy mirror was parked on 27 September to focus on the softphone and notes work; the staging copy on 28 September, with the review-before-merge workflow in place but the second container not yet set up. Nothing about either design depends on anything else. The alternatives (Power Automate flows per account, or a paid sync service) are listed under Considered and set aside below.
